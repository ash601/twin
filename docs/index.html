Twin

You on camera without filming. Upload a photo, add your voice, get a talking video.

Download free
  1. Unzip it.
  2. Double-click START.cmd (Windows) or START.command (Mac).
  3. Connect HeyGen at the top.
  4. Upload your photo → add your audio → Make video.

Needs Node.js and ffmpeg. Code: github.com/ash601/twin
