Twin

You on camera without filming. Pick how you look (a HeyGen avatar or a photo of you), give it your voice (record it, or type it), and HeyGen makes the video. It lands in the app as a real mp4.

What you need
  • Node.js 20 or newer: nodejs.org (the LTS button).
  • ffmpeg, for your voice takes and thumbnails. Windows: winget install ffmpeg. Mac: brew install ffmpeg.
  • A HeyGen account, connected one of two ways (you pick in the app, and can switch any time):
Connect my HeyGen account
Uses your HeyGen subscription credits.

CLAUDE CODE REQUIRED This way, Claude Code makes the video for you with its HeyGen connector. Install Claude Code, then on claude.ai → Settings → Connectors turn on HeyGen and log in.

Paste API key
Pay per second from your HeyGen API balance. No Claude needed.

Get a key at platform.heygen.com → API keys and add some balance. Prices: platform.heygen.com/pricing.

4 steps
  1. Double-click START: START.cmd on Windows, START.command on a Mac. The first time it installs and builds (a couple of minutes), then the app opens at http://localhost:3960.
    Mac says it can't open it? Right-click START.command → Open → Open. Or in Terminal: chmod +x START.command.
  2. Connect HeyGen on the strip at the top. Pick one way. It shows ● Connected when it works. The account way also shows ● Claude found (or ✕ Claude not found, with an install link).
  3. Bring in your looks: press Refresh my looks. Or use your own photos: press Add photos and drop pictures of you into the folder (or onto the screen).
  4. Make it: pick a look, record your voice (or type a script and pick the voice that reads it), pick the shape, and press Make with Claude / Make video. It shows up under Your videos in a few minutes.
Prompt for Claude (account way)

The app shows this with your folder filled in, with Copy and Open Claude here buttons. You can also paste it into Claude Code yourself, opened in the my-twin folder:

Set up my Twin app in this folder (<your Twin folder>/my-twin): check the HeyGen connector is connected, list my HeyGen looks and voices into looks.json and voices.json (follow CLAUDE.md here), and tell me if anything is missing.
What you get
  • Real mp4s of you talking, in 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1, with your room or a green screen.
  • Place it: see your video sitting over any screenshot (green keyed out live).
  • Everything stays on your computer in my-twin/: photos, voice takes, videos, and your key (if you pasted one). Deleted videos go to my-twin/.trash.
  • Turn off (top right) stops the app. Double-click START to open it again.
